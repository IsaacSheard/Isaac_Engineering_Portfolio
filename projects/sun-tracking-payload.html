360° Sun-Tracking Payload

A vision-guided tracking system integrating cameras, stepper motors, microcontrollers, and satellite communication to achieve continuous 360-degree sun tracking, built as a NASA Space Grant Consortium Research Intern at the University of Minnesota.

RoleNASA Space Grant Consortium Research Intern
TimelineJun 2023 – Aug 2023
HardwareSteppers, tracking camera, microcontroller
01

What I Built

I developed a sun-tracking payload for a high-altitude balloon flown during a solar eclipse. I added software features that improved the payload's tracking reliability and extended it to full 360° tracking.

I also programmed the microcontrollers for stratospheric data collection and payload control, integrating sensors for attitude, angular rate, magnetic field, acceleration, GPS, temperature, and pressure.
