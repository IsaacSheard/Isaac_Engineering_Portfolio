What I Built

I owned the mechanical, electrical, compute, sensor, and autonomy integration for two autonomous rovers. On the second platform, I went from chassis delivery to the first autonomous SLAM map in 9 days, and to field-proven autonomous navigation in 6 weeks. I also built the sim-to-real infrastructure that deploys autonomy across both mechanically different rovers and simulation, so 6 autonomous behaviors transfer between platforms without rewriting the underlying autonomy logic.

Beyond the hardware, I was entrusted with technical hiring and onboarding: I interviewed master's-level engineering candidates, trained new hires on the rover/autonomy stack, and own the team's core robotics tooling.
